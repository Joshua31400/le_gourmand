Pasta Carbonara

★★★★☆ 4.5 / 5

Description

This is the detailed description of the recipe and its story. A true Roman classic, born in the kitchens of central Italy during the Second World War.

Information

Country:
Italy
Type:
Main Course
Diet:
Vegetarian
Time:
30 min

Ingredients

  • 400g spaghetti
  • 200g guanciale or pancetta
  • 4 egg yolks
  • 100g Pecorino Romano
  • Black pepper to taste
  • Pinch of salt

Preparation

  1. Cook the spaghetti in a large pot of salted boiling water until al dente.
  2. Meanwhile, cut the guanciale into small batons and fry in a pan until crispy.
  3. In a bowl, whisk together egg yolks, grated Pecorino, and a generous amount of black pepper.
  4. Reserve a cup of pasta water before draining the spaghetti.
  5. Toss the pasta into the pan with the guanciale, remove from heat, and add the egg mixture. Mix vigorously, adding pasta water little by little to create a creamy sauce.
  6. Serve immediately with extra Pecorino and pepper.
